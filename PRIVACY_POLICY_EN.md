# Duo Angle Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-10-17**
**Contact: To be completed before release with a public developer contact email**

Duo Angle displays hinge angles on compatible devices and lets users manage target angles. This policy describes the app's information handling.

## On-device processing and storage

The system provides hinge measurements, which the app uses at runtime to display the angle and detect target-angle events. User-created target angles and their enabled state are stored locally in `UserDefaults`. Duo Angle does not send these measurements or targets to a Duo Angle server. Deleting a target or uninstalling the app removes the corresponding local app data; device backup behavior depends on the user's device and Apple settings.

## Purchases and third-party services

The app uses Apple StoreKit to load an optional voluntary-support consumable and process transactions. The app receives product and transaction-verification results from StoreKit to complete transactions. Apple processes App Store account, payment, and transaction information under its applicable privacy policy and terms. Duo Angle does not receive or store payment-card numbers. The app has no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs and no first-party network API.

## Collection, sharing, and logs

Based on the current code, Duo Angle does not upload measurements, target angles, or device identifiers to a developer server, perform advertising tracking, or request camera or microphone access. The app writes limited hinge-state changes and StoreKit error types without measurement values to the operating-system log for diagnostics; the operating system manages those logs. The app does not sell user data to other companies.

## Children's privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information to the developer may contact the developer using the public email above (which must be completed before release).
